# Dependencies and provenance

The eight curated integration skills are rewritten operating guidance from the
originating factory installation. Their source mapping is in
[the skill inventory](docs/skill-inventory.md). Historical skill libraries and
third-party adaptations are not included in this public snapshot.

Hermes Agent, Docker, Python, PyYAML, pytest, pytest-cov, Ruff, and pip-audit are
separate projects distributed under their respective licenses. This repository
does not vendor their code. Installing dependencies does not change their licenses.

This is an independent community project, not an official Hermes Agent release.
